Privacy Policy

This is the privacy policy for First Church of Springfield. The goal here is to be straightforward about what information we collect and why.

What gets collected

When you email the church or send a message through this site, you share your name, email address, and whatever details you include. We use that information only to respond to you. Nothing else.

If you subscribe to our newsletter, your email address goes to our email provider so occasional news from the church can reach you. It is not shared with anyone else.

What doesn't happen

  • Your information is never sold.
  • There is no ad targeting or tracking pixels on this site.
  • Traffic is measured with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page visits without setting cookies or identifying individual visitors.

Unsubscribing from the newsletter

Every email includes an unsubscribe link at the bottom. Click it and you're off the list right away. If you run into any trouble, email directly and it'll be handled promptly.

Questions or data requests

If you have questions about how your information is handled, or want your data removed, reach out at . You'll hear back within a few business days.

This policy was last reviewed in May 2026 and will be updated if anything meaningful changes about how this site handles your data.
